# Flip — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** February 17, 2025

---

Flip ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users ("you" or "your"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your personal and financial information when you use the Flip application and related services, including our web application and any mobile applications.

By creating an account or using Flip, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

- Account registration information (name, email address, phone number)
- Authentication credentials (securely hashed passwords)
- Budget preferences and financial categories you configure within the app

### 1.2 Financial Information via Plaid

When you connect a financial institution through Plaid, we may receive the following information with your explicit consent:

- Account names, types, and balances
- Transaction history (merchant names, amounts, dates, categories)
- Institution names and routing information

We access this data solely through the Plaid API in accordance with Plaid's terms of service and security requirements. **We do not store your banking login credentials.**

### 1.3 Automatically Collected Information

- Device type and browser or app information
- IP address and general geographic location
- Usage analytics (pages or screens visited, features used, session duration)

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- To provide and operate the Flip budgeting application
- To display your financial accounts, balances, and transactions within the app
- To help you manage and categorize your spending according to your budget
- To authenticate your identity and secure your account (including SMS and email verification codes)
- To communicate with you about your account, service updates, and support requests
- To improve and develop new features for the application

**We do not sell your personal or financial information to third parties. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.**

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

| Third Party | Data Shared | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Plaid | Plaid access tokens | Securely connect to your financial institutions |
| Supabase | All application data | Database hosting and authentication services |
| Twilio (or SMS provider) | Phone number | Deliver SMS verification codes for account security |
| Hosting Provider(s) | Application logs | Web and API hosting and delivery |

We may also disclose your information if required by law, regulation, or legal process, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or the public.

## 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:

- Encryption of data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher
- Encryption of all data at rest using AES-256
- SMS-based one-time password verification at signup and before sensitive financial actions, with email OTP as a fallback
- Row Level Security (RLS) database policies ensuring users can only access their own data
- Server-side API authentication verifying user identity on every request
- Regular security assessments and dependency vulnerability scanning

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to maintaining safeguards consistent with industry best practices.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your personal and financial data only for as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. Please refer to our Data Retention and Disposal Policy for detailed retention schedules. When your data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted in accordance with our disposal procedures.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

- **Access:** You can view all personal and financial data stored in your Flip account at any time through the application.
- **Correction:** You can update your account information through the application settings.
- **Deletion:** You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us. Account deletion is processed within 30 days.
- **Disconnect:** You may disconnect linked financial institutions at any time through the application. Upon disconnection, we revoke the associated Plaid access token and cease receiving new data from that institution.
- **Portability:** You may request an export of your data in a machine-readable format.

## 7. Consent

We obtain your explicit consent before collecting, processing, or storing your financial data. When you connect a financial institution through Plaid Link, you are presented with a clear explanation of what data will be accessed and how it will be used. You may withdraw your consent at any time by disconnecting your financial institutions or deleting your account.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Flip is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a user is under 18, we will promptly delete their account and associated data.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy within the application and updating the effective date. Your continued use of Flip after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
